How to Use

  1. Adjust current magnitude using the slider
  2. Set current direction using X, Y, Z controls
  3. Rotate view: Left click + drag
  4. Zoom: Mouse wheel
  5. Pan: Right click + drag
Right Hand Rule:

Point your right thumb in the direction of current (yellow arrow). Your fingers naturally curl in the direction of the magnetic field (blue lines).
